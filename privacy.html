Privacy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

The short version

This website has no sign-up, no contact form, no cookies and no analytics. I only get information you choose to send me by email.

What I collect

  • When you email me: your name, email address, and anything you include, such as links to your channel or episodes.
  • When you hire me: the video and audio files you send, and payment details handled by the payment provider (PayPal, Wise or Payoneer). I don't see or store your card details.

How I use it

Only to reply to you, make and deliver your clips, and handle payment. I don't sell or share your information, and I don't add you to mailing lists.

How long I keep it

Source files are deleted within 30 days after the last delivery. Emails and invoices are kept as long as needed for records. You can ask me to delete your information at any time.

Services this website uses

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts and a small scrolling script loads from unpkg.com, so those services receive your IP address when the page loads.
