Questions

What do I need to send?

A YouTube link to the episode, or the video file. Video podcasts and interviews work best. I watch it and pick the moments myself.

How fast do I get clips?

The 3 free Shorts within 48 hours. On a plan, new clips arrive every week, usually within 48 hours of each episode.

What if I don't like them?

Tell me what to change. Every clip includes one round of changes. If the free sample isn't for you, keep it anyway.

Do you post for me?

I send ready-to-post files with a title, description and hashtags for each platform. Posting stays with you, so you keep full control of your accounts. I never need your passwords.

Who owns the clips?

You do. Once a clip is paid for, it's yours to use however you want. The free ones are yours too.

Which platforms?

YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok and Facebook Reels. Same vertical file, with copy written for each.

Can you match my style?

Yes. Send a clip you like, or your colors and fonts, and the captions and titles will follow them.

Do you use AI?

I use tools to transcribe and to speed up the edit, but I choose every moment and check every caption by hand. Fully automatic clippers are exactly what this replaces.
